# Interview Q&A: Consulting at Tech Mahindra for Westpac

When an interviewer asks about your time at **Tech Mahindra**, they want to understand your consulting relationship with the client (**Westpac**) and the scope of the projects you handled. 

Because you were there for 3 years, the best strategy is to say you worked for **one major client (Westpac)** on **one massive enterprise application (Bank Guarantee)**, but that application was delivered in **three distinct phases (or sub-projects)**.

Here is exactly how to answer variations of this question.

---

## Question 1: "How many projects did you work on during your 3 years at Tech Mahindra?"

**The Answer:**
"For my entire 3-year tenure at Tech Mahindra, I was dedicated to a single, major client: **Westpac Bank**. 

I worked exclusively on a massive enterprise portal called the **Bank Guarantee Application**. Because it was such a large, multi-year digital transformation initiative, we operated it as three distinct project phases:
1. **Year 1 Project:** Building the core foundational REST APIs and integrating Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) securely into the bank's SSO.
2. **Year 2 Project:** Building the complex Workflow and State Engine, handling strict document approvals and compliance audit logging.
3. **Year 3 Project:** Scaling the application. We modernized the Dashboard APIs using GraphQL and built automated background workers for PDF legal document generation.

So technically it was one massive application, but I treated it as three entirely different scaling challenges over the years."

---

## Question 2: "As a consultant from Tech Mahindra, what was your relationship like with the Westpac team?"

**The Answer:**
"It was a highly integrated, agile environment. Even though I was an employee of Tech Mahindra, I functioned as a core part of the Westpac engineering pod. 

We followed strict Agile methodologies. Every morning, I attended stand-ups alongside internal Westpac developers, product owners, and business analysts. I was responsible for taking Jira stories directly from the Westpac backlog, developing the Node.js backend logic, and demoing my APIs during the sprint reviews."

---

## Question 3: "Did you bounce around between different clients at Tech Mahindra?"

**The Answer:**
"No, I was fully dedicated to the Westpac account. Westpac places a very high premium on domain knowledge—it takes months just to fully understand banking compliance, security protocols, and internal systems like their Single Sign-On and AWS architecture. Because of my deep understanding of the Bank Guarantee domain and my performance in Year 1, Tech Mahindra and Westpac leadership kept me anchored as a core backend developer on this platform for the full 3 years."

---

## Question 4: "If you were on one application for 3 years, did your technical learning stagnate?"

**The Answer:**
"Not at all. In fact, being on a project for 3 years allowed me to experience the complete software lifecycle, which is something you often miss if you jump projects every 6 months. 

In the beginning, I was just building basic Express.js REST APIs. But by Year 2, I had to learn how to solve complex database locking issues when multiple bankers tried to approve the same document. By Year 3, the database was getting slow, so I had to learn how to implement **GraphQL** and **Redis** caching to optimize the dashboards. I stayed with the project through its entire evolution from a simple MVP (Minimum Viable Product) to a high-traffic, highly scaled enterprise system."

---

## Question 5: "What happens if a different client application needs you? Do you help other teams?"

**The Answer:**
"While my primary dedication (about 90% of my time) was on the Bank Guarantee workflow, we did have a 'Center of Excellence' culture within our Tech Mahindra pod. If another Westpac team was struggling with a specific Node.js issue—for example, figuring out how to properly stream large AWS S3 file uploads without crashing their pod's memory—I would occasionally step in for peer reviews or pair-programming sessions to help them implement the solutions we had already perfected on our application."
